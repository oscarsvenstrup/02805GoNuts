03 / What surprised us

Links tell
different stories.

The directed view keeps two stories separate: being mentioned by many pages is different from mentioning many pages. Collapsing the arrows into pairs reduces 1,784 links to 1,434 relationships, because 350 pairs link in both directions.

The 17 isolates are easy to lose if the graph is built only from the edge list. Keeping the complete node roster makes those quiet characters part of the story.
